01 / MY MOTIVATION

Why I want to study frontend development

Turning ideas into experiences people can actually use.

I want to study frontend development because I am fascinated by the way a simple idea can become an accessible, useful website. Every day, people rely on digital services to communicate, learn, work and solve problems. I want to understand how those experiences are created—not only how they look, but how they work.

Learning HTML, CSS and JavaScript will give me a practical foundation for designing responsive pages, improving user experiences and building projects I can share with others. I am especially motivated by the combination of creativity and logical problem-solving: a developer must consider both the people using a website and the code behind it.

My goal is to develop strong fundamentals through structured teaching, feedback and hands-on projects. I see this course as an opportunity to move from curiosity and experimentation to confident, disciplined frontend development.

02 / MY POTENTIAL

Why I could become a good developer

A willingness to learn, test, improve and try again.

I believe I can become a good frontend developer because I approach new challenges with curiosity, patience and a willingness to improve. Web development requires attention to detail, the ability to break a problem into smaller steps and the determination to keep testing until a solution works. These are habits I want to strengthen through consistent practice.

I also value clear communication and the needs of the end user. A good website should not simply look impressive; it should be understandable, responsive and accessible to people using different devices.

I do not claim to know everything about development already. What I offer is a serious commitment to learning the fundamentals, asking thoughtful questions, accepting feedback and building real projects. I look forward to collaborating with classmates, learning from instructors and gradually creating work that demonstrates both technical skill and thoughtful design.
